Checked every day

Every page you save is checked once a day, automatically. A small mark tells you which still work, which have moved, and which are gone, with a way to find an archived copy.

Watches for trouble

Old websites sometimes change hands and turn into something else. Pagemarks notices new owners, harmful-site lists and pages that changed completely, and asks before opening them.

Calm and tidy

Folders, a Home page for your favorites, and Tidy up to file things one at a time. An optional AI helper, the Librarian, suggests folders; nothing moves until you say so.

Made for everyone

Large, clear text, plain words, a short guide, and family-friendly content rules. It works on computers, tablets and phones, and saves from your browser or your phone's Share menu.

Made by its first user, for the long haul

No ads, ever. Your information is never sold. Your Pagemarks are yours: download them anytime, in a file any browser can open.
