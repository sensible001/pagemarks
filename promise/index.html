Our promise

Last updated: November 24, 2026

In short: Pagemarks is made by someone who uses it every day and plans to for life. Your Pagemarks are yours, you can take them with you anytime, and if Pagemarks ever had to close, you'd hear about it well ahead of time.

Made by its first user

Pagemarks started as one person's way of organizing their own corner of the internet, and it's still used that way every day. It isn't a startup built to be sold. It's here for the long haul because its maker depends on it too.

Your Pagemarks are yours

  • You can download everything at any time from Settings → Export: an HTML file that any browser can import, with your folders, or a complete JSON backup.
  • No ads, ever. Your information is never sold or shared for marketing. The privacy policy has the details.

If things change

  • If Pagemarks ever had to close, you'd get at least 90 days' notice, by email and in the app. Export would keep working the whole time. Afterwards, your data would be deleted, never sold.
  • If someone else ever took over running Pagemarks, these promises would go with it, and you'd be told first, with time to leave and take your Pagemarks with you.
  • If prices ever change, you'd get at least 30 days' notice, and anything you've already paid for stays as it is.

Calm by design

Pagemarks doesn't follow you around the web, doesn't chase your attention, and never files or deletes anything without asking you. It looks after your links, and leaves the decisions to you.
