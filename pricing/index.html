Questions

Can I try it first?

Yes. Try Pagemarks with a sample library. No account is needed, and nothing you do in it is saved.

How does the Family plan work?

The person who pays invites family members by email from Settings. Everyone signs in with their own Google account and has their own private Pagemarks; nobody can see anyone else's. You can add or remove people anytime, up to 5 at once.

What are Librarian visits?

The Librarian is Pagemarks' optional AI helper. Each visit, it either files your Unfiled Pagemarks (up to 2,000), suggesting a folder for each, or reviews your whole library, suggesting folders to merge or rename and Pagemarks that seem misplaced. Nothing changes until you accept it.

Why is there a limit of 10,000 Pagemarks?

Every Pagemark is checked every day, and the limit keeps those checks quick for everyone. It's far more than everyday use; most people have a few hundred to a couple of thousand.

Can prices change?

Only with at least 30 days' notice, and what you've already paid for stays as it is. See our promise.

Can I leave anytime?

Yes. Download everything from Settings → Export first; it works with any browser. Your plan stays active until the end of the year you paid for.

Can I sign up now?

Not quite yet. Pagemarks is invitation-only for now.
